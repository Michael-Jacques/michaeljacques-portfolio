Verizon — An award-winning immersive activation for Verizon's New Business Incubation team: LED floor, LED walls and a 70" touch table driven by a physical puck.

With Left Field Labs as its storytelling collaborator, the Verizon New Business Incubation team is demonstrating how software solutions, built above Verizon's industry-leading network, can help usher customers into Industry 4.0.

The experiential activation included a fully immersive set with LED flooring and LED walls that connected to a 70" touch-screen table. When a user interacted with the table via a puck, it triggered the floor and wall to activate the video and motion content created by the team.
