Superblue — A mobile app that makes experiential art participatory, launching with an AR collaboration between artist JR and Niantic.

Superblue Art Gallery (Miami, FL) approached LFL with a unique challenge: how might they leverage the power of digital technology to make experiential art more accessible and participatory for all.

The idea was to build an app that would showcase the current exhibits within the gallery space, but more importantly, open the door for artists to create their own experiences through the digital space. The first was a partnership with the artist JR and the company Niantic, an AR experience that let users visit locations in real life and add photos and special notes for others to read.

Featured in Time Magazine and Complex Magazine.
