DataRobot — A designed-and-fabricated booth with demo stations, merch check-in and a mini theatre, toured to the biggest events in AI.

Left Field Labs was able to support DataRobot for run of shows with this booth that we designed and fabricated with a partner, taking it to some of the biggest events in the AI space.

This booth has areas for private demo stations, a check-in/merch stand and a mini theatre with seating. The team supported with merch, custom messaging, on-site support and full event support for each event. We also took DataRobot on the road to over 15 cities for the 9.0 product launch, and produced their fully virtual AIX event on Bizzabo.
